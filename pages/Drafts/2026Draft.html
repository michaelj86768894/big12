The 2026 GLFL Draft kicked off on Sunday, August 30, 2026, at 7:00 PM CDT, launching the league directly into the Dynasty Era. Transitioning away from the 2025 auction format, the 2026 draft featured a traditional linear (non-snaking) structure where each round repeated the same order—starting with the Miami Inferno at No. 1 overall based on reverse standings from the previous season, through to the defending champion Boston Brawlers at No. 12.

To keep draft positioning equal across the board, every franchise began selecting immediately in Round 1. Managers were permitted to retain up to 25 players from their 2025 rosters, meaning a team's total roster needs determined how long they stayed active in the draft. For instance, a team with 5 open roster spots participated in Rounds 1 through 5, while a team needing 7 players drafted through Round 7 until their roster was complete. The night set a high-stakes tone for the campaign, packed with strategic passes, aggressive target grabs, and key foundational picks.
