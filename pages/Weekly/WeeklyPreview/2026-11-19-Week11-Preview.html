GLFL WEEK 11 PREVIEW: HISTORY ON THE LINE, BROOKLYN'S 0-14 WATCH AND A SUPERIOR SHOWDOWN

By Julian Mercer | GLFL National • November 20, 2026

Four weeks remain in the GLFL regular season, and the stakes are getting higher by the minute. Milwaukee is coming off its first loss since opening week. Tucson just rewrote the league's scoring record books. Cedar Rapids is chasing something the franchise has never accomplished. And Brooklyn? Well, Brooklyn is chasing something nobody wants.

Meanwhile, the Superior Division continues to produce chaos, with Tucson, Northern Arizona, and Vegas all fighting for position as the playoff race tightens.

Six games. Twelve teams. And more than a few pieces of GLFL history hanging in the balance.

Milwaukee Mustangs vs. Cleveland Gladiators

Milwaukee favored by 35.5 points | Projection: 178.7 - 143.2

For eight consecutive weeks, nobody could beat the Milwaukee Mustangs.

Then Northern Arizona happened.

Milwaukee's 167.8-158.85 loss in Week 10 ended one of the most impressive winning streaks of the season and officially closed the door on a potential 13-1 finish.

Now GM Michael Bautch has a different challenge: making sure one loss doesn't turn into two.

The Mustangs enter Week 11 at 8-2, still holding the league's best record alongside Miami. A victory over Cleveland would give Milwaukee nine wins, matching its total from 2025.

But don't let Cleveland's projected 143.2 points fool you.

The Gladiators had been one of the league's hottest teams, winning five consecutive games before Vegas absolutely dismantled them 246.35-102.65 last week.

Cleveland has already demonstrated that it can compete with the league's best. The question is whether Josh Thurmer's team can respond after one of the most lopsided defeats in GLFL history.

Milwaukee needs a statement victory. Cleveland needs to prove last week was an aberration.

Mercer's Take: Milwaukee has been the league's most consistent team, but its Week 10 performance showed the Mustangs aren't invincible. Cleveland is dangerous enough to make this interesting, regardless of the projection.

Miami Inferno vs. Los Angeles Wildcats

Los Angeles favored by 0.9 points | Projection: 172.3 - 171.4

If you looked only at the standings, this matchup wouldn't seem particularly interesting.

Miami is 8-2. Los Angeles is 3-7.

Case closed, right?

Not even close.

The Wildcats hold a 14-9 all-time advantage over Miami and have won the last four meetings between these franchises.

That's right. Despite Miami's recent resurgence, the Inferno haven't been able to solve Los Angeles.

GM Richard Head enters Week 11 looking to end that streak, while Liz Bautch's Wildcats are desperately trying to salvage a disappointing season.

And there's another milestone looming for LA.

A loss would guarantee the Wildcats finish with a non-winning record for the first time since 2017, ending a remarkable run of sustained success.

For Miami, the stakes are different. The Inferno have already secured a winning season and are trying to position themselves for a deep playoff run.

The projections suggest this could be the closest game of the week, with LA holding a razor-thin 172.3-171.4 advantage.

Mercer's Take: Miami has been the better team all season, but four consecutive losses to LA aren't easy to ignore. If the Wildcats are going to play spoiler, this is their opportunity.

Philadelphia Soul vs. Brooklyn Bolts

Philadelphia favored by 8.2 points | Projection: 169.2 - 161.0

THE 0-14 WATCH IS OFFICIALLY ON

There are bad seasons. There are historically bad seasons.

And then there's what the Brooklyn Bolts are doing in 2026.

Cory Colvin's team enters Week 11 at 0-10, having already surpassed its previous franchise record for losses in a season.

Only one other team in GLFL history has ever started 0-11: the 2013 Boston Brawlers.

That Boston team eventually finished 0-14, becoming the only franchise in league history to complete an entire regular season without a victory.

Thirteen years later, Brooklyn is four losses away from joining them.

The Bolts have four opportunities remaining to avoid that fate, beginning with Philadelphia.

And if there's one reason for optimism in Brooklyn, it's the opponent.

Philadelphia has completely collapsed after opening the season 3-1. Brian Simmons' Soul have now lost six consecutive games, falling to 3-7 and watching their playoff aspirations evaporate.

This is a matchup between two franchises desperately searching for something positive.

For Philadelphia, it's about stopping the bleeding.

For Brooklyn, it's about avoiding immortality for all the wrong reasons.

Mercer's Take: Philadelphia is favored, but the Soul haven't won in six weeks. If Brooklyn is going to steal a victory this season, this might be one of its better opportunities.

Boston Brawlers vs. Vegas Knight Hawks

Boston favored by 4.0 points | Projection: 166.2 - 162.2

A rematch with history, momentum, and playoff implications.

Boston has won the last two meetings against Vegas, but the Knight Hawks still hold an 8-6 advantage in the all-time series.

And these teams couldn't have taken more different paths to their identical 5-5 records.

Boston opened the season 3-1 before stumbling through the middle of its schedule. The defending champions have struggled to establish consistency, but Chris Cholka's team remains firmly in the playoff conversation.

Vegas, meanwhile, started 0-4.

Then GM Dave Nguyen started making moves.

The acquisitions of Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Trey McBride, and other reinforcements completely transformed the Knight Hawks' offense.

Vegas has won five of its last six games, including a historic 143.7-point demolition of Cleveland in Week 10 - the largest margin of victory in GLFL history.

Now the Knight Hawks have an opportunity to move above .500 for the first time this season.

Boston has other plans.

At 166.2-162.2, the projections suggest a tight contest between two teams fighting for playoff positioning.

Mercer's Take: Vegas might be the most dangerous 5-5 team in the league. Boston has championship experience, but the Knight Hawks have momentum. This one could go either way.

Northern Arizona Wranglers vs. Chicago Slaughter

Chicago favored by 16.8 points | Projection: 189.2 - 172.4

One of the GLFL's most evenly matched historical rivalries takes center stage in Week 11.

Northern Arizona and Chicago are tied 8-8 in their all-time series.

But recent history has belonged almost entirely to the Slaughter.

Chicago has won six of the last seven meetings, establishing itself as a persistent thorn in Northern Arizona's side.

The problem?

This isn't exactly a vintage Chicago team.

At 4-6, Darrien Clay's Slaughter have struggled for consistency throughout the season, although their recent 262.4-point explosion against Los Angeles demonstrated just how dangerous this roster can be when everything clicks.

Northern Arizona enters at 6-4, fresh off a victory over Milwaukee that snapped the Mustangs' eight-game winning streak.

That win also strengthened the Wranglers' position in the crowded Superior Division race.

Jason Richmond's team has an opportunity to build momentum at precisely the right time.

But Chicago is projected to score 189.2 points, the second-highest total of any team this week.

If the Slaughter can reproduce even a portion of their Week 10 offensive explosion, Northern Arizona could be in trouble.

Mercer's Take: Chicago's recent dominance in this rivalry is real, but Northern Arizona just knocked off the league's top team. Something has to give.

Cedar Rapids Titans vs. Tucson Sugar Skulls

Tucson favored by 32.4 points | Projection: 208.1 - 175.7

This might be the most fascinating matchup on the Week 11 schedule.

Cedar Rapids is 7-3, chasing a milestone that has eluded the franchise throughout its history.

Tucson is 5-5, coming off the single greatest scoring performance the GLFL has ever seen.

And historically?

The Sugar Skulls have absolutely owned this matchup.

Tucson holds an 11-4 all-time advantage over Cedar Rapids, with the Titans' last victory coming in 2022.

For Mitch Smith and Cedar Rapids, the opportunity is enormous.

A victory would give the Titans their eighth win of the season, securing the franchise's fifth winning season and, more importantly, its first-ever stretch of consecutive winning seasons.

That's a milestone worth celebrating for a franchise that has struggled to maintain sustained success.

But Tucson isn't exactly in a charitable mood.

Mason Bautch's Sugar Skulls just obliterated Brooklyn 289.4-155.9, setting a new GLFL record for points scored in a single game, including the playoffs.

Patrick Mahomes, Jahmyr Gibbs, Ja'Marr Chase, and the rest of Tucson's star-studded offense reminded the league why the Sugar Skulls remain a championship threat despite their 5-5 record.

Tucson is projected for 208.1 points this week, easily the highest total on the board.

And there's still that lingering Jahmyr Gibbs storyline.

After weeks of trade rumors, a reported request to play for a contender, and Mason Bautch's public comments suggesting Milwaukee was a potential destination, Gibbs remains in Tucson following the trade deadline.

If the Sugar Skulls keep putting up 200-plus points, perhaps Gibbs won't need to leave Tucson to find the contender he's looking for.

Mercer's Take: Cedar Rapids has been one of the season's best stories, but Tucson's offensive ceiling is terrifying. The Titans will need a tremendous performance to overcome both the projections and the history.

Five Things to Watch in Week 11

1. Brooklyn's Date With History

A loss to Philadelphia would make Brooklyn just the second team ever to start 0-11, joining the infamous 2013 Boston Brawlers. The 0-14 watch continues.

2. Can Milwaukee Bounce Back?

The Mustangs' eight-game winning streak is over. A ninth win would match last season's total and reinforce Milwaukee's position among the championship favorites.

3. Cedar Rapids Chases Franchise History

The Titans need one victory to secure their first-ever back-to-back winning seasons. Standing in their way is a Tucson offense that just set the league's all-time scoring record.

4. The Superior Division Pressure Cooker

Northern Arizona, Tucson, and Vegas are separated by just one game in the standings. Every result matters, and none of these teams can afford to stumble down the stretch.

5. The End of LA's Winning-Season Streak?

One more loss guarantees Los Angeles its first non-winning season since 2017, despite the Wildcats entering as slight favorites against Miami.

The Final Word

Week 11 feels like one of those weeks where the league's future starts taking shape.

Milwaukee and Miami are trying to maintain their grip on the top of the standings. Cedar Rapids is chasing franchise history. Tucson is trying to turn record-setting offensive production into a late-season surge.

Vegas has gone from 0-4 disaster to legitimate playoff threat, while Boston is fighting to keep its championship defense alive.

And in Brooklyn, the countdown continues.

Four games remain for the Bolts to avoid joining the 2013 Boston Brawlers in the GLFL's most unwanted record book.

The playoff race is heating up. The history books are open. And Week 11 is ready to deliver.

- Julian Mercer, GLFL National
