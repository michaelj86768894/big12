GLFL WEEK 12 PREVIEW: A Thanksgiving Feast of Division Rivalries, Playoff Implications and Franchise History

By Julian Mercer | GLFL National • November 26, 2026

Happy Thanksgiving, GLFL fans! Grab yourself a plate of turkey, pile on the mashed potatoes, find a comfortable spot on the couch and make sure those fantasy lineups are set. The Great Lakes Fantasy League has prepared quite the Thanksgiving feast of its own.

Four divisional matchups. Three weeks remaining. Four unclaimed playoff spots. A potential division champion, two franchises staring at elimination and enough historical milestones to keep our statisticians busy through dessert.

Miami could celebrate its 100th regular-season victory, a division championship and just its second double-digit winning season in franchise history, all in the same weekend. Cedar Rapids and Milwaukee are separated by less than one projected fantasy point. Chicago is suddenly favored against one of the league's hottest teams.

And Brooklyn? Believe it or not, the Bolts enter Thanksgiving as favorites.

There's plenty to be thankful for this week, unless you're a general manager desperately trying to keep your season alive.

GAME OF THE WEEK: Milwaukee Mustangs (8-3) vs. Cedar Rapids Titans (8-3)

Division: Erie

Projected Score: Milwaukee 186.8, Cedar Rapids 186.0

Favorite: Milwaukee by 0.8

Forget the turkey. This is the main course.

Both franchises have secured postseason berths, both enter Week 12 with identical 8-3 records, and both have legitimate championship aspirations. Yet they arrive at this matchup traveling in very different directions.

Milwaukee has lost consecutive games after winning eight straight. The Mustangs have averaged just 152.35 points over their last two contests, a dramatic departure from the record-breaking offensive performances that defined their first half of the season.

Cedar Rapids, meanwhile, just defeated the second-ranked Tucson Sugar Skulls, reclaimed first place in the Erie Division and secured consecutive winning seasons for the first time in franchise history.

There is also some uncomfortable recent history for Milwaukee. The Titans have won two consecutive meetings against the Mustangs and are seeking their first season sweep of Milwaukee since 2019.

That would be a particularly impressive accomplishment considering the Mustangs have lost only three games all season.

Milwaukee needs its collection of offensive stars to rediscover the consistency that made this team so dangerous. CeeDee Lamb delivered 39.2 points last week, but the Mustangs simply haven't received enough production throughout the rest of their lineup.

Cedar Rapids enters with considerable confidence after Brock Purdy, Jonah Coleman and Bucky Irving combined for 115.65 points against Tucson. If the Titans can replicate anything close to that production, Milwaukee will have its hands full.

What's at Stake: Sole possession of first place in the Erie Division with two games remaining, positioning in the postseason and potentially a significant psychological advantage should these teams meet again in December.

Mercer's Pick: Cedar Rapids. The projections call this a virtual coin flip, but I'm siding with the team playing better football right now. Milwaukee has given me two consecutive reasons to question its consistency, while Cedar Rapids continues to give me reasons to believe.

Vegas Knight Hawks (5-6) vs. Northern Arizona Wranglers (6-5)

Division: Superior

Projected Score: Vegas 180.6, Northern Arizona 169.6

Favorite: Vegas by 11.0

Few games this weekend carry more direct postseason implications than this Superior Division showdown.

Northern Arizona enters Week 12 with a one-game advantage over both Tucson and Vegas. That advantage survived last week's loss to Chicago only because both of its closest divisional competitors also lost.

The Wranglers now have an opportunity to accomplish something this franchise hasn't managed in nearly a decade.

A victory would guarantee Northern Arizona a season of at least .500, something the franchise hasn't achieved since the 2015-2016 seasons. For a franchise that has endured years of losing football, that would represent a meaningful turning point.

Vegas, however, has no intention of participating in the celebration.

After recovering from an 0-4 start, the Knight Hawks find themselves below .500 again following a narrow defeat against Boston. Their last two performances illustrate just how difficult this roster is to evaluate: 246.35 points against Cleveland, followed by 154.55 against Boston.

Chuba Hubbard has been a major contributor, while Trey McBride provides Vegas with one of the most dangerous options at tight end. Northern Arizona will need Christian McCaffrey, Breece Hall and the rest of its offense to respond after last week's disappointing performance.

What's at Stake: Northern Arizona can create valuable separation in the Superior Division and guarantee a non-losing season. Vegas can pull even with the Wranglers at 6-6 and completely reshape the division race.

Mercer's Pick: Vegas. I don't entirely trust the Knight Hawks' consistency, but their offensive ceiling is difficult to ignore. I'm predicting an even more crowded Superior Division standings table by Tuesday morning.

Chicago Slaughter (5-6) vs. Cleveland Gladiators (7-4)

Division: Erie

Projected Score: Chicago 186.9, Cleveland 153.5

Favorite: Chicago by 33.4

If you had told me six weeks ago that Chicago would enter Thanksgiving as a 33-point favorite against Cleveland, I probably would have asked what was in your cranberry sauce.

But here we are.

The Slaughter have won four of their last five games after opening the season 1-5. Over the last two weeks, Chicago has scored a combined 484.15 points, an average of 242.08 per game.

Dak Prescott alone has contributed 137 fantasy points during that stretch. George Pickens, Emmett Johnson and the rest of Chicago's supporting cast have transformed this offense into one of the most intimidating units in the league.

ESPN currently gives Chicago a 42 percent chance of reaching the postseason. Considering where this franchise stood midway through October, that number is remarkable.

Cleveland deserves considerable respect of its own. The Gladiators have won six of their last seven games, including last week's victory over top-ranked Milwaukee. A win this weekend would secure the seventh winning season in franchise history.

It would also give Cleveland consecutive victories against Chicago for the first time since the 2021-2022 seasons.

Ashton Jeanty, Bryce Young and Calvin Ridley helped Cleveland recover from its historically lopsided Week 10 defeat. They'll likely need considerably more production this weekend if Chicago continues scoring at its recent pace.

What's at Stake: Cleveland can secure a winning season and strengthen its postseason position. Chicago can reach .500 and put enormous pressure on the teams occupying the final playoff spots.

Mercer's Pick: Chicago. Until somebody demonstrates an ability to slow down Dak Prescott and this offense, I'm not interested in betting against them. Darrien Clay might be engineering one of the most impressive midseason recoveries we've seen in recent years.

Miami Inferno (9-2) vs. Boston Brawlers (6-5)

Division: Michigan

Projected Score: Boston 175.7, Miami 161.5

Favorite: Boston by 14.2

Richard Head has an opportunity to make this Thanksgiving weekend one of the most memorable in Miami Inferno history.

With a victory over the defending champions, Miami would accomplish three significant franchise milestones:

  • Secure its fifth division championship and first since 2022.
  • Record its 100th regular-season victory in franchise history.
  • Reach 10 wins for just the second time in franchise history.

There is something particularly fitting about that final milestone.

The only previous Miami team to reach double-digit victories was the 2019 Inferno, which finished the regular season with 10 wins before capturing a GLFL championship. The general manager of that team? Richard Head.

After spending time away from the franchise, Head returned in 2024. Seven years after overseeing Miami's only double-digit winning season, he now stands one victory away from doing it again.

Considering the Inferno finished 3-11 just one season ago, Miami's turnaround has been nothing short of extraordinary.

But before anyone starts printing division championship merchandise, there's a rather significant obstacle standing in the way: the defending GLFL champions.

Boston has won consecutive games and enters Thanksgiving as a 14.2-point favorite. Jayden Daniels, Zay Flowers and a productive defensive unit helped the Brawlers defeat Vegas last week, and Chris Cholka's team remains firmly in the postseason conversation.

Miami has continued to find ways to win close games despite declining offensive production. The Inferno scored just 143 points against Los Angeles last week, with Cam Ward providing 40.7 of those points.

Eventually, Miami may need more than its remarkable ability to survive tight finishes.

What's at Stake: Miami can clinch the Michigan Division, reach 100 franchise regular-season wins and match its single-season victory record. Boston can move to 7-5, keep the division race alive and significantly strengthen its bid to defend the championship.

Mercer's Pick: Boston. Miami's ability to win close games has been impressive, but the Inferno's recent scoring performances concern me. I think the Brawlers make Richard Head wait at least one more week for his historic celebration.

Brooklyn Bolts (1-10) vs. Los Angeles Wildcats (3-8)

Projected Score: Brooklyn 164.4, Los Angeles 154.5

Favorite: Brooklyn by 9.9

Brooklyn fans, you have something extra to be thankful for this year.

Not only did the Bolts finally win a football game last weekend, but they now enter Week 12 as the projected favorite against Los Angeles.

After spending virtually the entire season discussing whether Brooklyn would become the second 0-14 team in GLFL history, we're suddenly discussing the possibility of consecutive victories.

Joe Burrow scored 41.9 points last week, while Malik Nabers, Tetairoa McMillan and DJ Moore combined for 75.7. Brooklyn's 201.10-point performance against Philadelphia was a reminder that this roster possesses considerably more talent than its record suggests.

For Los Angeles, however, this matchup is about something much more serious.

The Wildcats are facing the possibility of missing the postseason for the first time since 2018. A loss to Brooklyn would officially end their playoff hopes, regardless of what happens elsewhere in the league.

Los Angeles has already been guaranteed its first non-winning season since 2017, bringing an end to eight consecutive winning campaigns. Missing the postseason would mark another significant departure from the sustained success this franchise has enjoyed.

Josh Allen and James Cook III remain capable of producing explosive performances, but Los Angeles has struggled to assemble enough production throughout its lineup.

Brooklyn has nothing to lose. Los Angeles has everything to lose. That is a dangerous combination for the Wildcats.

What's at Stake: Brooklyn can earn consecutive victories and potentially eliminate another team's postseason hopes. Los Angeles must win to keep its already slim playoff chances alive.

Mercer's Pick: Brooklyn. That's right. I'm officially picking the Bolts to win a football game. Thanksgiving miracles apparently come in all shapes and sizes.

Philadelphia Soul (3-8) vs. Tucson Sugar Skulls (5-6)

Projected Score: Tucson 206.4, Philadelphia 181.3

Favorite: Tucson by 25.1

Philadelphia's season is hanging by a thread, and the team holding the scissors happens to possess one of the most explosive offenses in GLFL history.

Tucson enters Week 12 as the only franchise projected to exceed 200 points. Despite its losing record, the Sugar Skulls remain second in the GLFL Power Rankings and are just two weeks removed from establishing the league's all-time single-game scoring record.

Patrick Mahomes, Jahmyr Gibbs, Derrick Henry, Amon-Ra St. Brown, Ja'Marr Chase and Rashee Rice represent an extraordinary collection of offensive talent. Yet somehow, this team still finds itself fighting for a playoff berth.

Last week's defeat against Cedar Rapids was another frustrating chapter in Tucson's inconsistent season. The Sugar Skulls scored 185.45 points but received just 8.1 from Chase on Monday night, falling short of a crucial victory.

Philadelphia, meanwhile, has spent the last several weeks watching its postseason aspirations disappear. Last week's loss to previously winless Brooklyn was arguably the lowest point of the Soul's season.

There is also a peculiar historical trend at work here. Since 2019, Philadelphia has alternated between making and missing the playoffs. After qualifying last season, the Soul are now on the verge of continuing that pattern in 2026.

Philadelphia hasn't defeated Tucson since 2023. To extend its season, the Soul will need C.J. Stroud, Chris Olave and the rest of their offense to reverse that recent history.

What's at Stake: Tucson can reach .500 and remain firmly in contention for both the Superior Division championship and a playoff berth. Philadelphia must win to avoid immediate elimination.

Mercer's Pick: Tucson. The Sugar Skulls have too much offensive firepower, and the urgency surrounding their postseason situation should be considerable. Philadelphia's alternating playoff appearance trend looks destined to continue.

The Final Word

Thanksgiving is a time to appreciate the things we have. For some GLFL franchises, that means a guaranteed postseason berth. For others, it means a winning record, a chance at a division championship or simply the relief of finally having a victory to celebrate.

Brooklyn fans know exactly what I'm talking about.

But for Philadelphia and Los Angeles, this weekend could mark the unofficial beginning of the offseason. For Chicago, Tucson and Vegas, every remaining game feels increasingly like a playoff contest. And for Milwaukee and Cedar Rapids, Thanksgiving weekend offers an opportunity to establish supremacy in arguably the league's toughest division.

As for Miami, Richard Head has an opportunity to add another memorable chapter to a franchise whose greatest successes have already come under his leadership.

Enjoy the food. Enjoy the football. Enjoy the time with your families. And for the love of everything holy, double-check your starting lineups before kickoff.

Happy Thanksgiving from all of us at GLFN. We'll see you on the other side of the weekend.
