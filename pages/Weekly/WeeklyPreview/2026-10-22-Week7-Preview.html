GLFL WEEK 7 PREVIEW: No. 1 vs. No. 2, Father vs. Son and a Historic Milestone Within Reach

By Julian Mercer | GLFL National • October 22, 2026

The two highest-ranked teams in the GLFL are about to collide. No. 1 Milwaukee brings a five-game winning streak into a showdown with No. 2 Tucson, but the Sugar Skulls have won five consecutive meetings against the Mustangs. And there's a family connection that makes this rivalry unlike any other: Milwaukee GM Michael Bautch and Tucson GM Mason Bautch are father and son. Elsewhere, Los Angeles GM Elizabeth Bautch is chasing her 100th career victory, Brooklyn is searching for its first win, and Vegas has a chance to extend its remarkable dominance over Cedar Rapids.

Seven weeks into the season, the Great Lakes Fantasy League is delivering a matchup worthy of the championship stage.

Milwaukee and Tucson are not merely the top two teams in the latest Power Rankings.

They're also the only two teams still averaging more than 200 points per game, chasing a scoring standard no franchise has ever sustained for a full season.

And they know each other extremely well.

Tucson owns a 13-7 all-time advantage over Milwaukee, including five consecutive victories dating back to 2023.

Since Mason Bautch took over the Sugar Skulls, Tucson is 8-2 against Michael Bautch's Mustangs.

That dominance includes eliminating Milwaukee from the 2024 playoffs.

So while Milwaukee enters as the league's No. 1 team, there's a compelling argument that Tucson remains its most dangerous opponent.

The rest of the schedule offers plenty of intrigue, too.

Philadelphia and Miami renew a divisional rivalry. Cleveland looks to continue its winning streak against winless Brooklyn. Northern Arizona gets another opportunity to challenge a historically difficult opponent in Los Angeles.

Boston faces Chicago, while Cedar Rapids attempts to reverse years of frustration against Vegas.

Six games.

Multiple milestones.

And one matchup that could reshape the entire championship conversation.

NO. 2 TUCSON SUGAR SKULLS (4-2) vs. NO. 1 MILWAUKEE MUSTANGS (5-1)

Projected: Tucson 210.6 | Milwaukee 195.8

All-Time Series: Tucson leads 13-7

Recent History: Tucson has won five straight meetings; 8-2 against Milwaukee since Mason Bautch took over

The Main Event: Father vs. Son, No. 1 vs. No. 2

There are rivalries built on geography.

There are rivalries built on championship history.

And then there's Milwaukee versus Tucson.

Michael Bautch versus Mason Bautch.

Father versus son.

The two best teams in the GLFL, according to the latest Power Rankings, are about to settle another chapter of a rivalry that has become increasingly one-sided.

Milwaukee enters Week 7 ranked No. 1 with a 5-1 record and a Power Index of 4894.93.

Tucson sits second at 4-2 with a Power Index of 4770.40.

The Mustangs have won five consecutive games since their opening-week loss.

The Sugar Skulls, meanwhile, are coming off a humiliating 216.20-157.60 defeat to Vegas.

On paper, Milwaukee appears to have the momentum.

History tells a different story.

Mason Has Owned This Matchup

Tucson leads the all-time series 13-7.

More importantly, the Sugar Skulls have won five consecutive meetings against Milwaukee dating back to 2023.

Since Mason Bautch took control of Tucson, the Sugar Skulls are an astonishing 8-2 against the Mustangs.

That stretch includes Tucson eliminating Milwaukee from the 2024 playoffs.

For a Milwaukee franchise with championship aspirations, the Sugar Skulls have become a recurring obstacle.

Michael Bautch has built one of the most productive teams in GLFL history.

But his son has repeatedly found ways to beat him.

And if Milwaukee wants to establish itself as the league's unquestioned championship favorite, ending this losing streak would be a powerful statement.

Two Historic Offenses Collide

Milwaukee and Tucson are the only two teams averaging more than 200 points per game through six weeks.

No GLFL franchise has ever completed a season at that pace.

Los Angeles established the previous single-season high at 191.0 points per game in 2025.

Both teams are currently ahead of that historic benchmark.

Tucson is projected to score 210.6 points this week.

Milwaukee is projected at 195.8.

That's a combined projection of 406.4 points.

And those numbers may only scratch the surface of what these rosters are capable of producing.

Milwaukee scored a league-record 278.60 points in Week 4.

Tucson responded with 254.70 against Boston in Week 5, one of the highest regular-season totals in league history.

These teams have already demonstrated the ability to produce extraordinary offensive performances.

Now they get to test those offenses against one another.

Can Tucson Recover From the Vegas Disaster?

The Sugar Skulls enter this matchup facing questions about their aggressive roster overhaul.

Mason completed two major trades in roughly a week, reshaping several key offensive positions.

The most recent deal sent Jaxon Smith-Njigba, Trey McBride and Bhayshul Tuten to Vegas for Derrick Henry, Ja'Marr Chase and Juwan Johnson.

In Week 6, Smith-Njigba and McBride combined for 51.20 points against Tucson.

Henry managed just 8.00 in his Sugar Skulls debut, while Chase was unavailable on his bye week.

Vegas won by 58.60.

Now Tucson has a chance to answer the criticism with a victory over the league's top-ranked team.

And Chase's return to availability gives Mason another offensive weapon that wasn't available in the Vegas matchup.

A win over Milwaukee would immediately change the conversation surrounding those trades.

A loss would leave Tucson at 4-3 and raise further questions about whether Mason's roster changes have disrupted a championship-caliber team.

Milwaukee's Chance to Rewrite the Rivalry

The Mustangs have plenty to prove themselves.

Their five-game winning streak is the longest active run in the league.

Their Power Index leads all twelve teams.

And their 5-1 record is tied with Cedar Rapids for the league's best.

But none of those accomplishments erase the five consecutive losses to Tucson.

Michael Bautch has an opportunity to end that streak while strengthening Milwaukee's claim to the No. 1 ranking.

For a franchise chasing another championship, this matchup carries significance beyond the standings.

It's a test of whether Milwaukee's historic offense can finally overcome its most troublesome opponent.

Mercer's Matchup to Watch: Milwaukee's offensive balance against Tucson's newly reconstructed roster. The Mustangs have won with both explosive performances and steady production. Tucson has the higher projection, but its lineup is still settling after multiple trades.

Mercer's Outlook: Tucson is favored by 14.8 points, and the historical advantage is overwhelming. But Milwaukee has been the league's most consistent winner over the last five weeks. If the Mustangs are ever going to break Mason's hold on this rivalry, this is an excellent opportunity. Expect a heavyweight battle with major implications for next week's Power Rankings.

PHILADELPHIA SOUL (3-3) vs. MIAMI INFERNO (4-2)

Projected: Philadelphia 181.2 | Miami 166.7

Season Series: Miami leads 1-0

Philadelphia Looks for Divisional Revenge

Philadelphia enters Week 7 with a familiar opponent and an opportunity to correct an earlier mistake.

The Soul already lost to Miami once this season.

Now they get another chance.

The two Michigan Division rivals split their season series in 2025, and Philadelphia will be hoping to repeat that result this year.

But the teams enter this matchup heading in different directions.

Miami is 4-2 despite a disappointing 138.65-point performance against Milwaukee in Week 6.

Philadelphia is 3-3 after being demolished 210.90-135.20 by Los Angeles.

Both teams need a response.

Can Miami Rediscover Its Offense?

The Inferno scored 220.80 points against Tucson in Week 4 and 243.65 against Northern Arizona in Week 5.

Then the offense collapsed against Milwaukee.

Drake Maye continued producing with 45.15 points, but the supporting cast struggled.

Miami's 166.7-point projection suggests another competitive matchup, but not necessarily the offensive explosion Richard Head's team has demonstrated it can produce.

Philadelphia, meanwhile, is projected at 181.2.

That makes the Soul a 14.5-point favorite despite entering with the worse record.

For Brian Simmons, the opportunity is significant.

A victory would bring Philadelphia back above .500 and even the season series against a divisional rival.

A loss would drop the Soul to 3-4 and give Miami a season sweep.

Mercer's Matchup to Watch: Philadelphia's offensive supporting cast against Miami's ability to rebound. The Soul need better production from players like Bijan Robinson and Garrett Wilson, while Miami needs more help around Maye.

Mercer's Outlook: Philadelphia is favored, but Miami has already beaten the Soul once this season. The Inferno's ceiling is considerably higher than its Week 6 performance suggests. Expect a competitive divisional rematch with meaningful playoff implications.

CLEVELAND GLADIATORS (3-3) vs. BROOKLYN BOLTS (0-6)

Projected: Cleveland 192.4 | Brooklyn 162.7

All-Time Series: Brooklyn leads 10-7

Recent History: Brooklyn has won five of the last six meetings

Could Brooklyn Finally Break Through?

The GLFL's only winless team is running out of time.

Brooklyn enters Week 7 at 0-6, enduring the worst start in franchise history.

The Bolts have struggled to produce consistent offense, fallen behind early in the standings and become the subject of growing trade speculation.

But there is one reason for optimism.

Cleveland.

Brooklyn owns a 10-7 all-time advantage over the Gladiators and has won five of the last six meetings.

If Cory Colvin is looking for a favorable historical matchup to finally earn victory No. 1, this is about as encouraging as the schedule gets.

The problem?

Cleveland is playing its best football of the season.

Gladiators Riding Momentum

Josh Thurmer's team has won two consecutive games, improving from 1-3 to 3-3.

In Week 5, Cleveland erased a significant deficit behind Puka Nacua's 47.70-point performance to defeat previously undefeated Cedar Rapids.

In Week 6, the Gladiators survived Northern Arizona by just 0.30 points.

That victory was the ninth-closest finish in GLFL history, including playoffs.

Cleveland is now projected to score 192.4 points against Brooklyn.

The Bolts are projected at 162.7.

That's a 29.7-point advantage for the Gladiators.

Cleveland's recent offensive improvement has been driven by contributions from Bryce Young, Matthew Golden, Kyle Pitts Sr. and Nacua.

Brooklyn continues to receive excellent production from Malik Nabers, but the rest of the roster has struggled to deliver consistently.

Colvin has previously indicated that he may begin considering next season if the losses continue.

At 0-6, those conversations are becoming increasingly relevant.

Mercer's Matchup to Watch: Cleveland's improving offense against Brooklyn's historical dominance in this series. The Bolts have beaten the Gladiators repeatedly in recent seasons, but this version of Cleveland is gaining confidence.

Mercer's Outlook: Cleveland is the clear favorite based on current form. Still, Brooklyn's 5-1 record in the last six meetings provides an intriguing counterargument. If the Bolts are going to surprise somebody, history suggests Cleveland might be the opponent.

LOS ANGELES WILDCATS (2-4) vs. NORTHERN ARIZONA WRANGLERS (3-3)

Projected: Los Angeles 155.5 | Northern Arizona 175.2

All-Time Series: Los Angeles leads 12-6

Recent History: Los Angeles won nine of the previous ten meetings before Northern Arizona's Week 3 victory

History Favors LA, but the Wranglers Have Already Changed the Script

Los Angeles has dominated Northern Arizona for years.

The Wildcats lead the all-time series 12-6 and had won nine of ten meetings entering the season.

But Northern Arizona finally broke through in Week 3.

Now the Wranglers have an opportunity to prove that victory wasn't an isolated result.

Northern Arizona enters at 3-3 after one of the most heartbreaking losses in league history.

The Wranglers scored 192.25 points against Cleveland in Week 6.

Cleveland scored 192.55.

A 0.30-point defeat.

Ninth-closest in GLFL history.

And the loss marked Northern Arizona's 100th regular-season defeat.

Jason Richmond's team now needs to regroup.

Liz Bautch Chases History

Los Angeles enters this matchup with an entirely different historical opportunity.

Wildcats GM Elizabeth Bautch is seeking her 100th all-time victory in her 161st career game.

She would become just the second general manager in GLFL history to reach 100 victories, joining Milwaukee's Michael Bautch.

That's a significant accomplishment for one of the league's most established managers.

And the opponent is familiar.

Los Angeles has enjoyed remarkable success against Northern Arizona, but the Wranglers' Week 3 victory proved the matchup is no longer automatic.

The Wildcats are coming off their strongest performance of the season, scoring 210.90 points against Philadelphia.

James Cook III delivered 33.30 points, Carnell Tate added 23.00 and Josh Allen contributed 38.85.

Those performances demonstrated the upside of a roster that has undergone significant changes.

But despite that explosion, Los Angeles is projected to score only 155.5 points this week.

Northern Arizona is favored at 175.2.

That's a 19.7-point advantage for the Wranglers.

If Liz wants her 100th victory this week, the Wildcats will need to outperform expectations.

Mercer's Matchup to Watch: Los Angeles' newly productive offensive additions against Northern Arizona's ability to recover from its heartbreaking Week 6 defeat. Both teams have demonstrated 190-plus-point potential recently.

Mercer's Outlook: Northern Arizona is favored, but Los Angeles has dominated this matchup historically and just scored more than 210 points. With a milestone victory on the line for Liz Bautch, this could become one of the week's most compelling games.

BOSTON BRAWLERS (4-2) vs. CHICAGO SLAUGHTER (1-5)

Projected: Boston 166.2 | Chicago 173.8

The Defending Champions Face a Dangerous Underdog

Boston is 4-2.

Chicago is 1-5.

So naturally, Chicago is favored.

Welcome to fantasy football.

The Slaughter enter Week 7 projected to score 173.8 points, compared with 166.2 for the defending GLFL champions.

That's a 7.6-point advantage for a team that has won just once in six games.

And if there's one thing Week 6 taught us, it's that records and projections don't always tell the same story.

Boston Looks to Build Momentum

The Brawlers recovered from their humiliating Week 5 loss to Tucson by defeating Brooklyn 191.80-136.60.

Trevor Lawrence erupted for 54.40 points, Jonathan Taylor added 29.10 and Colston Loveland contributed 20.50.

The performance helped Boston improve to 4-2 and maintain its position among the league's leading contenders.

Chicago, meanwhile, is coming off a 168.20-144.20 defeat to Cedar Rapids.

That loss carried historical significance.

The Slaughter reached 100 regular-season defeats, and GM Darrien Clay became the first Chicago manager to reach the milestone.

Clay has been the franchise's only GM since the league began in 2012.

The Slaughter have shown flashes of offensive potential, including a 202.65-point performance in their Week 5 victory over Brooklyn.

But consistency remains elusive.

Boston has the better record and stronger season-long resume.

Chicago has the higher Week 7 projection.

Something has to give.

Mercer's Matchup to Watch: Boston's high-ceiling offense against Chicago's unpredictable production. If Trevor Lawrence continues delivering elite performances, the Brawlers will be difficult to beat. But Chicago has already demonstrated the ability to score more than 200 points.

Mercer's Outlook: Chicago is favored by 7.6 points, making this one of the week's more intriguing projection-versus-record matchups. Boston should be considered the more reliable team, but the Slaughter have enough talent to make things uncomfortable for the defending champions.

CEDAR RAPIDS TITANS (5-1) vs. VEGAS KNIGHT HAWKS (2-4)

Projected: Cedar Rapids 172.2 | Vegas 174.5

All-Time Series: Vegas leads 14-6

Vegas Looks to Continue Its Domination of Cedar Rapids

On the surface, this should be a straightforward matchup.

Cedar Rapids is 5-1.

Vegas is 2-4.

The Titans are ranked third in the Power Rankings.

The Knight Hawks are ninth.

But history suggests Cedar Rapids has plenty to worry about.

Vegas owns a commanding 14-6 all-time advantage over the Titans.

Five of Cedar Rapids' six victories in the series came between 2012 and 2018.

Since then, the matchup has belonged overwhelmingly to Vegas.

And the Knight Hawks enter Week 7 with considerable momentum.

Can Vegas Make It Three Straight?

Dave Nguyen's team started the season 0-4.

Now Vegas has won two consecutive games.

The latest victory was its most impressive of the season.

The Knight Hawks demolished No. 1 Tucson 216.20-157.60 in Week 6, with newly acquired Jaxon Smith-Njigba scoring 33.40 points and Trey McBride adding 17.80.

Kirk Cousins contributed 40.65, while D'Andre Swift and Chuba Hubbard combined for 55.10.

Vegas suddenly looks like a team capable of challenging anyone.

Cedar Rapids, meanwhile, recovered from its first loss of the season by defeating Chicago 168.20-144.20.

Brock Purdy led the Titans with 35.00 points, while DeVonta Smith and Tommy Tremble combined for 46.10.

The Titans are tied with Milwaukee for the league's best record.

But the projections suggest another difficult matchup.

Vegas is favored 174.5-172.2.

That's a narrow 2.3-point advantage for the Knight Hawks.

The matchup also marks an interesting milestone for both general managers.

Cedar Rapids' Mitch Smith and Vegas' Dave Nguyen are each coaching their 50th career GLFL game this week.

One will have an opportunity to celebrate with a victory.

The other will have to wait for a better result.

Mercer's Matchup to Watch: Vegas' revamped offense against Cedar Rapids' consistency. The Knight Hawks have the higher projection and historical advantage, but the Titans have been one of the league's most reliable teams through six weeks.

Mercer's Outlook: This is closer than the standings suggest. Vegas has won two straight, dominated the historical series and just defeated the league's top-ranked team. Cedar Rapids is still 5-1 for a reason, but the Titans should not expect an easy afternoon.

Milestone Watch: History Could Be Made in Week 7

Beyond the six matchups, several general managers have significant career milestones on the horizon.

Richard Head Miami Coaching 50th career GLFL game.

Milwaukee's Michael Bautch is currently the only general manager in GLFL history to reach 100 all-time victories.

Elizabeth Bautch has a chance to join him this week.

The milestone would carry additional significance given the family connection among several of the league's most prominent franchises.

Meanwhile, Richard Head, Mitch Smith and Dave Nguyen each reach their 50th career game, a reminder of how quickly a general manager's tenure can begin to establish its own history.

Five Things to Watch in Week 7

  1. Can Milwaukee finally beat Tucson? The Mustangs are ranked No. 1, but Mason Bautch has won eight of ten meetings against his father since taking over the Sugar Skulls.
  2. Will Tucson's new roster deliver? After a disastrous debut against Vegas, Derrick Henry and Ja'Marr Chase have an opportunity to help justify Mason's aggressive trades.
  3. Can Brooklyn get off the board? The Bolts are 0-6, but they've beaten Cleveland in five of their last six meetings.
  4. Will Liz Bautch make history? The Los Angeles GM is one victory away from becoming just the second manager in GLFL history with 100 career wins.
  5. Is Vegas becoming a legitimate playoff threat? The Knight Hawks have won two straight, including a blowout of No. 1 Tucson, and now face a Cedar Rapids team they've historically dominated.

The Final Word

Week 7 has the potential to become one of the defining weeks of the 2026 GLFL season.

Milwaukee and Tucson will settle another chapter of their father-son rivalry, with the top two positions in the Power Rankings hanging in the balance.

Philadelphia and Miami will battle for divisional positioning.

Brooklyn will try to avoid an 0-7 start.

Los Angeles will attempt to deliver Elizabeth Bautch's 100th career victory.

Boston will face a Chicago team that is somehow favored despite having just one win.

And Cedar Rapids will attempt to overcome a Vegas franchise that has repeatedly had its number.

But make no mistake.

The spotlight belongs to Milwaukee and Tucson.

The No. 1 and No. 2 teams in the league.

Two historic offenses.

Five consecutive Tucson victories.

A father trying to end his son's dominance.

And a son trying to prove that last week's disaster was nothing more than a temporary setback.

This isn't just another regular-season matchup.

It's a measuring stick for two championship contenders.

And by the time Week 7 is over, we may have a much clearer picture of who truly belongs at the top of the GLFL.
