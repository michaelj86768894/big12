Lock In Your Keepers: The 2026 GLFL Draft Order is Set

By Marcus Vance | Senior GLFL Analyst • August 9, 2026

The summer heat is peaking, which can only mean one thing across the Great Lakes Football League: draft season has officially arrived.

With the 2026 campaign looming, the league office has formally locked in the calendar for the most critical weeks of the offseason...

Key Offseason Dates & Deadlines

  • Keeper Lock Deadline: Sunday, August 23rd @ 7:00 PM CST
    All 12 GMs must submit official keepers to the league office. No extensions.
  • 2026 GLFL Official Draft: Sunday, August 30th @ 7:00 PM CST
    Virtual war rooms open at 6:00 PM CST. Draft clock starts promptly at 7:00 PM CST.

The board is set. Miami holds the keys to the kingdom at Pick 1 after a grueling offseason, while defending champion Boston will sit back and watch the carnage unfold before making their opening selection at the turn.
