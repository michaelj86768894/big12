GLFL WEEK 10 PREVIEW: Milwaukee Eyes Playoff Clinch, Miami and Cedar Rapids Collide, and Tucson Faces a Must-Win Moment

By Julian Mercer | GLFL National • November 12, 2026

Five weeks. Seven playoff spots. Eight teams with postseason probabilities of at least 51 percent. And one league leader approaching the finish line faster than anyone expected. Week 10 of the Great Lakes Fantasy League has arrived, and the stakes are getting considerably higher.

Milwaukee enters the week at 8-1, with an opportunity to match last season's win total and potentially clinch a postseason berth. Miami and Cedar Rapids square off in a battle of 7-2 contenders. Vegas and Cleveland meet with both franchises looking to surpass their 2025 win totals.

Meanwhile, Tucson faces winless Brooklyn in what should be the easiest matchup on the schedule. But after losing five of its last six games, nothing about the Sugar Skulls feels guaranteed anymore.

And for the teams fighting to stay alive, the calendar is becoming an increasingly uncomfortable reminder that time is running out.

Northern Arizona Wranglers (5-4) vs. Milwaukee Mustangs (8-1)

Projected Score: Milwaukee 183.7, Northern Arizona 167.2

Milwaukee's Playoff Countdown Begins

The league's top-ranked team has an opportunity to take another major step toward the postseason, but Northern Arizona has plenty of reasons to make this difficult.

Milwaukee enters Week 10 with the league's best record at 8-1 and a commanding 6,953.6 Power Index score. A victory would give the Mustangs nine wins, matching their total from the entire 2025 regular season.

More importantly, Milwaukee has a potential playoff-clinching scenario. A Mustangs victory, combined with a loss by Boston, Vegas, or Tucson, would secure a postseason berth under the league's current playoff scenarios.

That's remarkable considering there are still four more weeks to play after this one.

The Mustangs also hold a significant historical advantage in this matchup, having won six of their last seven meetings with the Wranglers.

But Northern Arizona isn't exactly arriving without momentum.

Jason Richmond's team just snapped a seven-game losing streak against Tucson, taking over first place in the Superior Division with a 212.30-point performance.

Jordan Love delivered 59.90 fantasy points in that victory, while Christian McCaffrey contributed 28.70.

The challenge for Northern Arizona is sustaining that production against a Milwaukee team that has been the league's most consistent winner.

Milwaukee also enters the matchup dealing with the season-ending neck injury to Michael Wilson, which creates another question at wide receiver.

Still, the Mustangs have repeatedly demonstrated that they can overcome personnel problems. Kenneth Walker III and CeeDee Lamb combined for 81.80 points last week, providing more than enough firepower to defeat Brooklyn.

Northern Arizona needs this game to protect its division lead. Milwaukee wants to take another step toward securing its playoff future.

Mercer's Edge: Milwaukee. The Mustangs are favored by 16.5 points, and their recent dominance in this rivalry is difficult to overlook. But Northern Arizona has enough upside to make this interesting.

Vegas Knight Hawks (4-5) vs. Cleveland Gladiators (6-3)

Projected Score: Vegas 182.1, Cleveland 156.2

Two Turnaround Stories. One Critical Matchup.

If you told somebody after Week 4 that Vegas and Cleveland would enter Week 10 as two of the league's most intriguing playoff contenders, they probably wouldn't have believed you.

Yet here we are.

Vegas started the season 0-4 before rattling off four consecutive victories. Although the Knight Hawks suffered a narrow loss to Miami last week, their four wins have already matched their entire 2025 total.

A victory over Cleveland would give Dave Nguyen's team its fifth win and officially surpass last season's mark.

Cleveland has an equally impressive story to tell.

The Gladiators opened 1-3 and have since won five consecutive games, climbing to 6-3. Last week's 223.05-point explosion against Los Angeles matched Cleveland's 2025 win total.

A sixth consecutive victory would move the Gladiators beyond that mark as well.

Cleveland's recent offensive production has been extraordinary. Bryce Young, Puka Nacua, and Matthew Golden combined for 133.00 fantasy points in Week 9 alone.

But despite Cleveland's five-game winning streak, ESPN currently favors Vegas by 25.9 points.

That's a substantial projection against one of the hottest teams in the league.

Vegas will be looking for a rebound from Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Trey McBride, who combined for just 28.50 points in last week's loss to Miami.

With both teams fighting for playoff positioning, this could become one of Week 10's defining games.

Mercer's Edge: Vegas, based on the 25.9-point projection advantage. But Cleveland's five-game winning streak makes the Gladiators an extremely dangerous underdog.

Boston Brawlers (4-5) vs. Philadelphia Soul (3-6)

Projected Score: Boston 180.4, Philadelphia 158.9

Two Franchises Searching for Answers

There was a time earlier this season when Boston and Philadelphia looked like comfortable playoff contenders.

Those days feel increasingly distant.

Boston started 3-1 and has since gone 1-4, dropping below .500 following a 222.60-146.90 defeat against Cedar Rapids.

The loss was Boston's 100th in franchise history, an unfortunate milestone for the defending GLFL champions.

Chris Cholka's team still has a realistic opportunity to reach the postseason, but the Brawlers cannot afford to keep giving away games.

Philadelphia's situation is even more troubling.

Brian Simmons' Soul opened 3-1 before losing five consecutive games. Their latest defeat was particularly painful: a 4.25-point loss to Chicago that could have been avoided with a different defensive lineup decision.

C.J. Stroud scored 65.20 fantasy points in that game, but even that wasn't enough.

Now Philadelphia enters Week 10 at 3-6, facing a defending champion equally desperate for a victory.

ESPN favors Boston by 21.5 points.

For the Brawlers, this is an opportunity to stabilize a season that has gone sideways.

For Philadelphia, it might be one of the final chances to keep postseason hopes alive.

Mercer's Edge: Boston. The defending champions have the stronger statistical profile and the projection advantage. But neither team has inspired much confidence lately.

Los Angeles Wildcats (3-6) vs. Chicago Slaughter (3-6)

Projected Score: Los Angeles 171.8, Chicago 148.0

Playing for Pride, and Whatever Hope Remains

Two 3-6 teams. Two frustrated general managers. And one game that both desperately need to win.

Los Angeles enters Week 10 coming off a disastrous 223.05-127.15 loss to Cleveland.

The Wildcats have shown flashes of competitiveness, including their Week 8 upset over Tucson, but sustained success has remained elusive.

And the historical implications are becoming significant.

Even if Los Angeles wins its remaining five games, the Wildcats would finish 8-6, meaning they'll fail to reach nine victories for the first time since 2018.

It's a remarkable departure from the consistency Elizabeth Bautch has established over the years.

Chicago, meanwhile, enters the matchup with a little more momentum after defeating Philadelphia 183.85-179.60.

Dak Prescott, Christian Watson, and Arvell Reese all delivered impressive performances in that victory.

But at 3-6, Darrien Clay's Slaughter remain in a difficult position.

ESPN favors Los Angeles by 23.8 points, despite Chicago being the team coming off a victory.

Neither franchise can afford another defeat if it wants to maintain even a remote chance of reaching the playoffs.

Mercer's Edge: Los Angeles. The Wildcats have the stronger projection, but Chicago has demonstrated that it can produce a competitive score when its key players deliver.

Miami Inferno (7-2) vs. Cedar Rapids Titans (7-2)

Projected Score: Miami 172.5, Cedar Rapids 141.2

The Battle of the 7-2 Contenders

This is the matchup I've been waiting for.

Two teams with identical 7-2 records. Two franchises chasing major milestones. And two organizations trying to establish themselves as legitimate championship threats.

Miami enters Week 10 having already exceeded its three-win total from the entire 2025 season.

A victory would give Richard Head's Inferno their seventh winning season in franchise history and their first since 2022.

That's a remarkable turnaround for a team that finished 3-11 just one year ago.

Cedar Rapids has an equally compelling milestone within reach.

A victory would secure the Titans' fifth winning season and their first back-to-back winning seasons in franchise history.

For Mitch Smith, that would represent a major step forward for an organization that has struggled to sustain success across multiple seasons.

Cedar Rapids enters this game riding high after scoring 222.60 points against Boston.

Brock Purdy delivered 47.60 fantasy points, Bucky Irving added 34.50, and Carson Schwesinger contributed 30.75.

Miami, meanwhile, escaped Week 9 with a narrow 155.60-149.00 victory over Vegas.

The Inferno have consistently found ways to win, even when their scoring output hasn't matched their early-season explosions.

ESPN currently favors Miami by 31.3 points.

That's a surprisingly large projection gap considering Cedar Rapids just delivered one of Week 9's strongest performances.

Both teams are chasing history. Both teams are chasing playoff positioning. And both teams have legitimate championship aspirations.

Mercer's Edge: Miami. The Inferno have the projection advantage, but Cedar Rapids' explosive Week 9 performance makes this a much more intriguing matchup than the numbers initially suggest.

Brooklyn Bolts (0-9) vs. Tucson Sugar Skulls (4-5)

Projected Score: Tucson 212.2, Brooklyn 155.3

Tucson's Get-Right Game... Or Another Disaster?

On paper, this should be straightforward.

Tucson is projected to score 212.2 points against a Brooklyn team that has yet to win a game.

The Sugar Skulls are favored by 56.9 points, the largest projection advantage of Week 10.

But after watching Tucson lose five of its last six games, can anybody honestly call this a guaranteed victory?

Mason Bautch's team started the season 3-0 and looked like a championship favorite.

Since then, the Sugar Skulls have gone 1-5, surrendered the Superior Division lead, and become the subject of increasingly loud trade speculation.

The Jahmyr Gibbs situation continues to generate headlines, with reports suggesting the star running back wants to join Milwaukee.

Bautch has publicly acknowledged the possibility of sending Gibbs to the Mustangs, although no deal has been completed.

For now, Tucson needs Gibbs and the rest of its talented roster focused on winning games.

Brooklyn, meanwhile, is facing another painful historical milestone.

Last season's 4-10 finish was the worst in Bolts franchise history and the only time Brooklyn had ever lost 10 games in a season.

A loss to Tucson would give the Bolts their second consecutive 10-loss campaign.

At 0-9, Cory Colvin's team is already enduring its worst start in franchise history.

Yet Brooklyn has occasionally demonstrated offensive potential. Joe Burrow scored 54.25 points against Milwaukee last week.

The problem has been finding enough production around him.

For Tucson, this game represents an opportunity to regain some confidence.

For Brooklyn, it's another chance to finally end a nightmare losing streak.

Mercer's Edge: Tucson. The Sugar Skulls are overwhelming favorites. But given their recent collapse, Mason Bautch should probably avoid making any victory speeches before Monday night.

Trade Deadline Passes Without a Blockbuster

After weeks of aggressive roster moves, blockbuster deals, and enough trade rumors to keep the GLFL Network working overtime, Wednesday's trade deadline came and went with surprisingly little excitement.

Several general managers were actively pursuing deals, including Milwaukee's Michael Bautch, Tucson's Mason Bautch, and Chicago's Darrien Clay. Multiple offers were circulated, but none resulted in a completed transaction.

For Tucson, the silence was particularly noteworthy. The Sugar Skulls have been at the center of trade speculation for weeks, including the highly publicized Jahmyr Gibbs saga. Despite rumors of a potential move to Milwaukee, and Mason Bautch's own comments suggesting an offer could be coming, Gibbs remains in Tucson as the deadline passes.

Meanwhile, Milwaukee will move forward with its existing roster despite the season-ending injury to wide receiver Michael Wilson, and Chicago will have to find its answers internally.

After one of the league's more entertaining stretches of trading, the deadline ultimately delivered more conversations than transactions.

The phones have stopped ringing. The rosters are set. Now it's time to see which general managers made the right decisions — and which ones will spend the offseason wishing they'd pulled the trigger.

Five Things to Watch in Week 10

1. Can Milwaukee Clinch?

The Mustangs have a chance to secure their postseason future with four weeks still remaining. A victory, combined with a loss by Boston, Vegas, or Tucson, would put Milwaukee into the playoff field.

2. Which 7-2 Team Makes History?

Miami is pursuing its seventh winning season and first since 2022. Cedar Rapids is seeking its fifth winning season and first consecutive winning seasons in franchise history. Only one can win this matchup.

3. Can Cleveland Keep Its Streak Alive?

Five straight victories have transformed Cleveland from a struggling 1-3 team into a 6-3 contender. The Gladiators now face a Vegas team that is favored by nearly 26 points.

4. Is the Superior Division Still Wide Open?

Northern Arizona leads at 5-4, while Tucson and Vegas sit one game behind at 4-5. With the Wranglers facing Milwaukee and the other two teams favored in their matchups, the division could tighten considerably this weekend.

5. Can Brooklyn Avoid Another 10-Loss Season?

The Bolts have never lost 10 games in consecutive seasons. A defeat against Tucson would change that, and leave Brooklyn still searching for its first victory of 2026.

Mercer's Final Word

The first nine weeks of the GLFL season have produced remarkable turnarounds, unexpected collapses, blockbuster trades, and some truly historic performances.

Now the stakes are changing.

Milwaukee is chasing a playoff clinch. Miami and Cedar Rapids are chasing franchise milestones. Cleveland and Vegas are trying to prove their recent success is sustainable.

Northern Arizona is defending a division lead. Tucson is trying to stop a collapse. Boston is fighting to protect its championship defense.

And Brooklyn?

Brooklyn would just like to win a football game.

Five weeks remain. Seven teams will reach the postseason.

And starting this weekend, every victory becomes a little more valuable.
