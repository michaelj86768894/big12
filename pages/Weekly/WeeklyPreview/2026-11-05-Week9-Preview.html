GLFL WEEK 9 PREVIEW: Milwaukee Chases History, Tucson Fights for Survival and Vegas Looks to Keep Rolling

By Julian Mercer | GLFL National • November 5, 2026

Eight weeks have reshaped the Great Lakes Fantasy League. Milwaukee stands alone at 7-1, Vegas has erased an 0-4 start, Cleveland has won four consecutive games, and Tucson's once-commanding lead in the Superior Division has disappeared. Now Week 9 brings six matchups filled with historical streaks, division implications and potential franchise milestones. From a bizarre 13-game pattern between Milwaukee and Brooklyn to a showdown between two of the league's hottest teams, the second half of the season is beginning to take shape.

PHI-CHI | TUC-NAZ | MIA-LV | MKE-BKN | CLE-LA | CR-BOS | Watch List

PHILADELPHIA SOUL (3-5) vs. CHICAGO SLAUGHTER (2-6)

Projected: Philadelphia 166.3 | Chicago 162.4

Two struggling franchises. One opportunity to stop the bleeding.

Philadelphia opened the season 3-1 and appeared ready to challenge in the Michigan Division. Four consecutive losses later, Brian Simmons' Soul have fallen to 3-5 and are desperately searching for answers.

Chicago hasn't been much better. Darrien Clay's Slaughter are 2-6 and coming off a historically embarrassing 241.45-109.25 defeat against Vegas, the second-largest margin of defeat in GLFL history.

The historical matchup is remarkably close. Philadelphia leads the all-time series 8-7, but Chicago has won four of the last five meetings. The Soul did win the most recent matchup, however, giving them a chance to build a modest winning streak against their longtime rival.

Philadelphia enters as a narrow 3.9-point favorite, suggesting this could be one of the week's closest contests.

For Simmons, the stakes are obvious. A fifth consecutive loss would push Philadelphia to 3-6 and make its promising start feel like a distant memory.

For Clay, a victory would give Chicago something it has struggled to find all season: momentum.

Mercer's Take: Philadelphia has the slightly better roster on paper, but recent history favors Chicago. This is a dangerous matchup for a Soul team that hasn't won since Week 4.

TUCSON SUGAR SKULLS (4-4) vs. NORTHERN ARIZONA WRANGLERS (4-4)

Projected: Tucson 213.2 | Northern Arizona 170.2

There may not be a more important game in the Superior Division this week.

Tucson and Northern Arizona enter with identical 4-4 records, but their recent struggles have opened the door for Vegas to join them in the division race.

Mason Bautch's Sugar Skulls started 3-0 before losing four of their next five games. The latest defeat came against Los Angeles, a 201.20-198.70 setback that continued Tucson's stunning slide.

Northern Arizona hasn't taken advantage. Jason Richmond's Wranglers started 3-1 but have gone just 1-3 since.

Something has to give.

Historically, Tucson has dominated this matchup. The Sugar Skulls lead the all-time series 13-8 and have won seven consecutive games against Northern Arizona.

That streak includes the 2022 Great Lakes Bowl, when Tucson defeated the Wranglers 185-121 to capture the championship.

Tucson is also the week's largest projected favorite, carrying a massive 43-point advantage.

But projections haven't protected the Sugar Skulls from their recent struggles.

The pressure surrounding Bautch has only increased following the blockbuster trade that sent Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Trey McBride to Vegas. Both players have become major contributors for the Knight Hawks, while Tucson has continued losing.

Rumors have even circulated that star running back Jahmyr Gibbs wants to be traded to a contender, although no formal trade request has been confirmed.

A victory would stabilize Tucson's season and extend its dominance over Northern Arizona.

A loss would make the situation considerably more uncomfortable.

Mercer's Take: Tucson has the talent, the projections and the historical advantage. But after losing four of five, the Sugar Skulls need to prove they can still finish games. Northern Arizona has a tremendous opportunity to change the direction of its season.

MIAMI INFERNO (6-2) vs. VEGAS KNIGHT HAWKS (4-4)

Projected: Miami 175.4 | Vegas 184.8

This is the matchup I've been waiting for.

Miami has quietly established itself as one of the league's most consistent teams, sitting at 6-2 and leading the Michigan Division.

Vegas, meanwhile, might be the hottest team in fantasy football.

After opening 0-4, Dave Nguyen's Knight Hawks have won four consecutive games, capped by a 241.45-point explosion against Chicago in Week 8.

The turnaround has been remarkable.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Trey McBride have transformed the Vegas offense since arriving from Tucson, while Nguyen continues adjusting his roster to address weaknesses.

The latest move sent running back J.K. Dobbins to Miami in exchange for wide receiver Josh Downs, giving the Knight Hawks additional receiving depth.

Now the trade partners meet.

Miami leads the all-time series 10-6, but the last five meetings have produced an unusual pattern: the teams have alternated victories, with Miami winning three and Vegas winning two.

If that trend continues, Vegas would be next.

The projections agree, favoring the Knight Hawks by 9.4 points.

For Miami, a victory would reinforce its status as a legitimate championship contender and slow the league's hottest turnaround.

For Vegas, a fifth consecutive victory would send an unmistakable message to the Superior Division.

Mercer's Take: Miami has the superior record, but Vegas has the momentum. This feels like a potential playoff preview, and the Knight Hawks have earned their status as the favorite.

MILWAUKEE MUSTANGS (7-1) vs. BROOKLYN BOLTS (0-8)

Projected: Milwaukee 206.4 | Brooklyn 160.3

On paper, this looks like the easiest prediction of Week 9.

Milwaukee is 7-1, owns the league's No. 1 Power Ranking, and has won seven consecutive games.

Brooklyn is 0-8, the worst start in franchise history.

The Mustangs are favored by 46.1 points.

So why should Milwaukee fans be nervous?

Because history has a very strange sense of humor.

Over the last 13 meetings dating back to 2016, Milwaukee and Brooklyn have followed an almost unbelievable pattern: Milwaukee wins one game, then Brooklyn wins the next two.

Repeat.

The most recent meeting? Milwaukee won.

If the pattern continues, Brooklyn is due.

And the overall history supports the idea that this rivalry is closer than the current standings suggest.

Brooklyn leads the all-time series 8-7, while Milwaukee holds a razor-thin scoring advantage of 134.3 points per game to Brooklyn's 133.0.

Fifteen meetings. Nearly identical scoring averages.

That's remarkable.

There's also a major milestone at stake for Milwaukee.

With a victory, the Mustangs would clinch their 12th winning season in the league's 14-year history. Milwaukee hasn't finished with a losing record since 2022.

Michael Bautch has built one of the GLFL's most consistently successful franchises, and the 2026 Mustangs are looking increasingly capable of making another championship run.

Brooklyn, however, has been agonizingly close to its first victory.

The Bolts lost to Cleveland by 3.65 points in Week 7 and Miami by just 2.00 points in Week 8.

That's consecutive defeats by a combined 5.65 points.

Cory Colvin's team is winless, but it hasn't stopped fighting.

Mercer's Take: Everything about the current season favors Milwaukee. Everything about the historical pattern says Brooklyn. If the Bolts are going to pull off the upset of the year, this would be one heck of a time to do it.

CLEVELAND GLADIATORS (5-3) vs. LOS ANGELES WILDCATS (3-5)

Projected: Cleveland 180.3 | Los Angeles 168.9

Cleveland has completely rewritten its season.

After starting 1-3, Josh Thurmer's Gladiators have won four consecutive games and climbed to 5-3.

The latest victory was a convincing 184.85-117.90 win over Philadelphia.

But Los Angeles presents a historical problem Cleveland hasn't solved in years.

The Wildcats lead the all-time series 10-5 and have won eight consecutive meetings against the Gladiators dating back to 2019.

Cleveland hasn't beaten Los Angeles during that stretch.

Elizabeth Bautch's Wildcats enter at 3-5 after defeating Tucson in Week 8, a victory that gave Bautch her 100th career GLFL win, including playoffs.

She became just the second GM in league history to reach the milestone, joining Milwaukee's Michael Bautch.

Los Angeles now has an opportunity to build on that historic victory while continuing its dominance over Cleveland.

The Gladiators, however, have plenty of motivation.

A fifth consecutive victory would move Cleveland to 6-3 and strengthen its postseason position considerably.

Cleveland is favored by 11.4 points, but recent head-to-head history suggests the matchup could be more difficult than the projections indicate.

Mercer's Take: Cleveland is playing its best football of the season, but eight straight losses to Los Angeles are impossible to ignore. The Gladiators have a chance to end one of the league's more frustrating active streaks.

CEDAR RAPIDS TITANS (6-2) vs. BOSTON BRAWLERS (4-4)

Projected: Cedar Rapids 171.6 | Boston 177.4

Two teams heading in very different directions.

Cedar Rapids is 6-2 and tied for the second-best record in the league.

Boston is 4-4 after opening the season 3-1.

The defending champions have managed just one victory in their last four games, and another loss would drop them below .500 for the first time this season.

There's also a historical milestone looming.

Boston enters Week 9 with 99 regular-season losses in franchise history.

A defeat against Cedar Rapids would make the Brawlers the latest franchise to reach 100 regular-season losses.

Chris Cholka would certainly prefer to postpone that distinction.

The Titans, meanwhile, have won four of their last six meetings against Boston.

Mitchell Smith's team bounced back from its Week 7 loss to Vegas with a 197.90-173.20 victory over Northern Arizona last week.

Boston is projected to win by 5.8 points, making this one of the week's closest matchups.

The Brawlers recently acquired Cooper Kupp and Brenton Strange from Cleveland, hoping to strengthen their roster for the second-half push.

But roster changes only matter if they translate into victories.

Boston has the championship pedigree.

Cedar Rapids has the better record.

And with the season entering its final stretch, both teams have plenty to play for.

Mercer's Take: Boston is a slight favorite, but Cedar Rapids has been the more reliable team this season. This could be a defining game for the defending champions.

FIVE THINGS TO WATCH IN WEEK 9

1. Can Milwaukee Clinch Another Winning Season?

The Mustangs are one victory away from their 12th winning season in 14 years. A win over Brooklyn would also extend their current winning streak to eight games.

2. Is Tucson's Superior Division Grip Finally Slipping?

Tucson has lost four of five games and now shares the division lead with Vegas and Northern Arizona. A loss to the Wranglers would further complicate an increasingly crowded race.

3. Can Vegas Win Five Straight?

The Knight Hawks have transformed an 0-4 disaster into a legitimate playoff push. A victory over 6-2 Miami would be their strongest statement yet.

4. Will Cleveland Finally Beat Los Angeles?

Eight consecutive losses against the Wildcats dating back to 2019. Four consecutive victories entering Week 9. One of those streaks will end.

5. Does Boston Reach 100 Regular-Season Losses?

The defending champions enter with 99 regular-season defeats and face a 6-2 Cedar Rapids team that has won four of their last six meetings.

THE FINAL WORD

Week 9 offers a fascinating contrast between the league's established powers and its emerging challengers.

Milwaukee continues chasing another historic season, but Brooklyn's bizarre head-to-head pattern provides an unexpected subplot.

Tucson is desperately trying to stop a slide that has turned the Superior Division into a three-team race.

Vegas is looking to prove its remarkable turnaround is no fluke.

And Cleveland has an opportunity to extend its winning streak while ending years of frustration against Los Angeles.

The standings are tightening, the playoff race is heating up, and the historical milestones are piling up.

Welcome to Week 9.
