GLFL WEEK 7 POWER RANKINGS: Milwaukee Pulls Away, Vegas Surges and Tucson Faces Growing Turmoil

By Marcus Vance | GLFL National • October 29, 2026

Milwaukee has separated itself from the field. The Mustangs are 6-1, winners of six straight, and just dismantled their biggest rival in a No. 1 versus No. 2 showdown. But the real movement is happening behind them: Miami climbs into the top three, Northern Arizona surges to fourth, Vegas jumps three spots after another 200-point explosion, and Cedar Rapids tumbles four places. Meanwhile, Tucson remains No. 2 despite three losses in four games—and an unconfirmed trade rumor surrounding star running back Jahmyr Gibbs.

Seven weeks into the GLFL season, one team has earned the right to look down at everyone else.

Milwaukee.

The Mustangs didn't just beat Tucson in Week 7. They ended a five-game losing streak against the Sugar Skulls, scored 202.40 points and won by 59.20.

That victory pushed Milwaukee to 6-1, extended its winning streak to six games and opened a substantial gap at the top of the Power Rankings.

But below the Mustangs, the standings and the Power Index are telling very different stories.

Tucson is still second despite its recent struggles. Miami has climbed into third after completing a season sweep of Philadelphia. Northern Arizona jumped into fourth after producing the week's highest score.

And Vegas?

The Knight Hawks have gone from 0-4 to 3-4, winning three straight and dismantling Tucson and Cedar Rapids in consecutive weeks.

Their rise is one of the biggest stories of the season.

On the other side of that equation, Cedar Rapids has fallen from third to seventh after a 73-point defeat to Vegas.

The Titans are still 5-2.

But their Power Index suggests they have considerably more to prove.

The Power Index is based on total points scored, winning percentage and performance against the weekly league median. It rewards sustained production, not just victories.

Here are the Week 7 rankings.

1. MILWAUKEE MUSTANGS (6-1) — 5706.24

Previous Rank: 1 (--) | Week 7: Defeated Tucson 202.40-143.20

The Undisputed No. 1

There is no longer a serious argument about which team deserves the top spot.

Milwaukee settled that debate on the field.

The Mustangs entered Week 7 ranked No. 1, but Tucson had dominated the rivalry for years.

Five consecutive Sugar Skulls victories.

An 8-2 record for Mason Bautch against his father since taking over Tucson.

A playoff elimination in 2024.

All of it ended with Milwaukee's 59.20-point victory.

Jared Goff scored 39.05 points, Javonte Williams contributed 29.50, CeeDee Lamb added 29.40 and Alex Singleton delivered 27.25 on defense.

The Mustangs produced 202.40 points without relying on a single 50-point superstar performance.

That's what makes this team so dangerous.

Milwaukee can beat opponents with explosive individual performances, but it can also overwhelm them with production throughout the lineup.

The Mustangs now own a Power Index of 5706.24, a staggering 549.36 points ahead of second-place Tucson.

They're the league's only 6-1 team.

They've won six straight.

And they're still chasing the GLFL's single-season scoring record.

Michael Bautch has built the team everyone else is trying to catch.

Vance's Verdict: Milwaukee isn't simply No. 1 because of its record. The Mustangs are No. 1 because they've demonstrated the highest combination of scoring production and consistent winning. After finally beating Tucson, the biggest remaining question is whether anyone can slow them down.

2. TUCSON SUGAR SKULLS (4-3) — 5156.88 ▲ 0

Previous Rank: 2 (--) | Week 7: Lost to Milwaukee 202.40-143.20

Still No. 2. But for How Much Longer?

Tucson is the most controversial team in this week's rankings.

And frankly, it should be.

The Sugar Skulls have lost three of their last four games.

They've suffered consecutive blowout defeats to Vegas and Milwaukee.

Their record has fallen to 4-3.

And yet, they remain No. 2 in the Power Index.

How?

Because the ranking formula still recognizes the extraordinary scoring production Tucson delivered earlier this season.

A 254.70-point performance against Boston in Week 5 doesn't disappear just because the team has struggled recently.

But the gap between Tucson's statistical resume and its current performance is growing.

Against Milwaukee, Amon-Ra St. Brown scored 32.20 points, Aaron Rodgers added 27.20 and Jahmyr Gibbs contributed 24.30.

Ja'Marr Chase delivered 21.20 in his return from a bye week.

But Derrick Henry managed just 7.90 points, Isaiah Likely scored 4.20 and Tucson's defense combined for only 19.50.

The result was another lopsided loss.

And Now There's a Gibbs Rumor?

As if the on-field struggles weren't enough, Tucson is now facing questions about the future of star running back Jahmyr Gibbs.

GLFL Network's Alex Ramirez reported Tuesday that Gibbs has requested a trade and informed GM Mason Bautch that he wants to play for a contender.

The report remains unconfirmed, and Tucson has not announced any transaction involving Gibbs.

But the timing couldn't be worse.

The Sugar Skulls were the league's top-ranked team just two weeks ago.

Now they're 4-3, struggling to find consistency after multiple blockbuster trades, and reportedly dealing with dissatisfaction from one of their most productive players.

Gibbs has continued producing on the field, including 24.30 points against Milwaukee.

So if the report proves accurate, Mason would be facing another major roster decision at a time when his team desperately needs stability.

Tucson remains second because of its season-long statistical performance.

But another disappointing week could make that position increasingly difficult to defend.

Vance's Verdict: The Power Index says Tucson is No. 2. Recent results say this team has serious problems. Mason Bautch needs to stop the slide before a once-promising championship season becomes defined by questionable trades, disappointing performances and unnecessary distractions.

Miami Reclaims Its Place Among the Elite

That turnaround deserves recognition.

Miami's Power Index climbs to 4983.26, moving the Inferno ahead of Northern Arizona's previous position and into the top three.

More importantly, Miami has the record to match its ranking.

Vance's Verdict: Miami's Week 6 performance was an exception, not the rule. Richard Head has built an explosive offense around Drake Maye, and the Inferno have earned their place among the championship favorites.

Boston remains fifth.

Vance's Verdict: Boston has the talent to defend its title, but championship teams cannot afford to leave winnable games on the table. Cholka's Week 7 lineup decision cost the Brawlers a victory, and the standings are becoming too competitive for mistakes like that.

6. VEGAS KNIGHT HAWKS (3-4) — 4558.86

Previous Rank: 9 ( ▲ 3) | Week 7: Defeated Cedar Rapids 204.35-131.35

The Hottest Team Nobody Saw Coming

Three weeks ago, Vegas was 0-4.

Today, the Knight Hawks are 3-4, riding a three-game winning streak and ranked sixth in the GLFL.

That's a remarkable transformation.

And it has happened almost entirely since Dave Nguyen completed his blockbuster trade with Tucson.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Trey McBride have been extraordinary.

Against Cedar Rapids, Smith-Njigba scored 39.30 points and McBride delivered 31.10.

Combined, they produced 70.40 points.

In two games with Vegas, the duo has scored 121.60 points.

Meanwhile, Derrick Henry has scored just 15.90 in his first two games with Tucson.

Again, it's too early to declare a permanent winner of the trade.

But the early returns are overwhelmingly favorable for Vegas.

Kirk Cousins added 40.35 points against Cedar Rapids, while Blake Cashman delivered 29.00 and D'Andre Swift contributed 24.00.

Vegas scored 204.35 points, its second consecutive 200-point performance.

And the Knight Hawks have now beaten Tucson and Cedar Rapids in back-to-back weeks.

Those aren't bottom-feeding opponents.

Those are teams that entered the season's first half among the league's best.

Vegas jumps three places to No. 6 with a Power Index of 4558.86.

That's a massive move.

And based on recent form, it may not be the last.

Vance's Verdict: Vegas is no longer a feel-good comeback story. The Knight Hawks are becoming a legitimate contender. Dave Nguyen has assembled an offense capable of overwhelming quality opponents, and the rest of the league should be paying attention.

The Final Word: Milwaukee's League to Lose?

The Week 7 Power Rankings reveal a league that is beginning to separate into distinct groups.

Milwaukee stands alone at the top.

Tucson, Miami, Northern Arizona and Boston remain within striking distance of one another in the Power Index.

Vegas is rapidly closing the gap.

Cedar Rapids and Cleveland have strong records but significantly lower scoring indexes.

Philadelphia, Los Angeles and Chicago are searching for consistency.

And Brooklyn is still searching for its first victory.

The most intriguing question isn't whether Milwaukee deserves to be No. 1.

That debate was settled against Tucson.

The question is whether the Mustangs can maintain this extraordinary pace for the rest of the season.

Tucson has already demonstrated how quickly a dominant team can lose momentum.

Cedar Rapids has discovered that a strong record doesn't guarantee a high Power Index.

And Vegas has shown that a team written off after four weeks can completely transform its season.

There's still plenty of football left.

But for now, the hierarchy is clear.

Milwaukee is No. 1.

Everyone else is chasing.

And if the rest of the league wants to change that, they'll have to do more than talk about it.

They'll have to beat the Mustangs.
